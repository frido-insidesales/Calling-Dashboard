How to use this dashboard

  1. Pick a section from the launcher: Business Overview, Calling Performance, Demo Insights, Agent Performance or Marketing Intelligence. Switch sections with the bar at the top, Prev / Next, or the cards at the end of each page. All sections takes you back to the launcher.
  2. Pick the dates with Today / Yesterday / MTD (month to date) / YTD (financial year to date), or Custom to choose your own From and To dates.
  3. Narrow it down with Vertical and Team (top left). Active filters show as chips under the menu; click × on a chip, or Reset, to clear them.
  4. Hover the small i next to a number to see what it means. Click a team, agent or product row to drill in. Every section has PDF and Excel buttons.

How FRT is calculated
  1. FRT (First Response Time) = time of the first call on a lead − the time the lead was created in LSQ. Shown as hh:mm:ss.
  2. Working hours (default): only time between 10 AM and 8 PM counts, every day of the week. A lead created at 8:45 PM and called at 10:15 AM the next morning has an FRT of 15 minutes. Switch to Clock time to count every minute.
  3. Each lead is counted once, using its earliest call (by any agent). The FRT belongs to the agent who made that first call.
  4. Leads are grouped by the date the lead was created, so “Today” means leads created today. Data starts from 1 April 2026.
  5. Not called yet = leads created in the range with no call so far; they are shown under the lead owner and are not part of the median.
  6. Median = the middle FRT (half the leads were faster). Average is shown alongside. Times are exact to the second.

How demos are counted
  • Read straight from the two demo sheets (Video Call Demo Request – Mobility and – Office Chairs, i.e. Ergo Furniture) every 10 minutes. Nothing is copied into the dashboard sheet.
  • Date = Demo Date. Agent = Mobility Sales Agents (Mobility) or ISD agent (Ergo Furniture). Demo executive = Demo Agent / VC Agent.
  • Requested = every demo request in the sheet. Done = the final count: Demo Done "Yes" (Mobility) or Status "Completed" (Ergo Furniture). Pending = requested − done (marked not done / cancelled, or no result filled in yet).
  • Target per agent per month: . For part of a month it is pro-rata: "This month" up to the 19th counts 19/31 of the month target.
  • Sales from demos = Sale Done "Yes" (Mobility) or an Order id filled in (Ergo Furniture).
  • With all teams, totals include every demo in the sheets, also from people not in the team lists (shown as "Not in team lists").
