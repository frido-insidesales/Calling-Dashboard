How FRT is calculated
  1. FRT (First Response Time) = time of the first call on a lead − the time the lead was created in LSQ. Shown as hh:mm:ss.
  2. Working hours (default): only time between 10 AM and 8 PM counts, every day of the week. A lead created at 8:45 PM and called at 10:15 AM the next morning has an FRT of 15 minutes. Switch to Clock time to count every minute.
  3. Each lead is counted once, using its earliest call (by any agent). The FRT belongs to the agent who made that first call.
  4. Leads are grouped by the date the lead was created, so “Today” means leads created today. Data starts from 1 April 2026.
  5. Not called yet = leads created in the range with no call so far; they are shown under the lead owner and are not part of the median.
  6. Median = the middle FRT (half the leads were faster). Average is shown alongside. Times are exact to the second.
